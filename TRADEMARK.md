- Use the software, read the source, and modify it, under the terms of the

- Refer to the project by name to state a plain fact - "a fork of Amazing
  Codex GUI", "compatible with Amazing Codex GUI", "a patch for Amazing Codex

- Submit changes back to this repository.

## What you may not do

- Publish a fork or a derivative work under the name Amazing Codex GUI, under

  your own ID, and your own logo.
- Use the name or logo in a way that suggests your fork is this project, or
  that the author endorses, sponsors, or maintains it.
- Remove, alter, or obscure the copyright, license, or authorship notices in
  the software, or the attribution shown in the plugin's own interface. The

If you distribute a fork, say clearly and near the top of your description that
it is an independent modified version and not the original.
